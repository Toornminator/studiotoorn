# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Affluent visitors and residents of the Costa del Sol (Marbella, Estepona, Sotogrande, Málaga) looking for a private chef: villa holiday groups, couples celebrating something, small companies, and locals who want the "Toorn aan de deur" weekly menu. They browse on phones by the pool and on laptops in the evening. Trilingual audience: English default, Spanish (Andalusian-warm), Dutch (plain, intimate). The job to be done: decide "is this the chef I want in my kitchen?" and book a date.

## Product Purpose

toornattable.com is the portfolio and booking site of Nick Toorn, a Michelin-trained private chef. One long narrative page (story, services, cookbook with 21 recipes, travels, events, weekly menu, booking form) plus per-recipe pages. Success = a booking enquiry through the contact form or WhatsApp. The site IS the proof of craft: a visitor should feel the evening before they book it.

## Brand Personality

Honest, raw, intimate. Anthony Bourdain meets Marco Pierre White, told as an old-school tattoo flash poster: warm paper, heavy slab type, hand-scrawled red notes, a fork for a cursor. First person, short sentences, fragments welcome. Not corporate, not catering. One chef, one table, a memory.

## Anti-references

- Corporate catering sites: stock chefs plating microgreens, "exquisite culinary journeys" copy.
- Blue/purple tech gradients, glassmorphism, neon glow, dark-mode-by-default SaaS aesthetics.
- Over-lit, over-styled food photography.
- Generic restaurant-template sites (hero slider, Lato/Playfair, gold script "Reserve Now").
- Em dashes. Anywhere. Hard editorial rule.

## Design Principles

1. **Paper, not screen.** Everything should feel printed, pasted, stamped or scrawled onto warm paper: grain, hairlines, polaroids, stickers.
2. **The menu is the theatre.** The page is paced like an evening with the chef: arrival, stories, the meal, the goodbye. Drama is allowed where the narrative earns it.
3. **Accents like seasoning.** Tattoo-red carries almost all emphasis; a little goes a long way. Mustard, jade, navy are pinches, not pours.
4. **Hand of the maker.** Imperfection is the luxury: rotated polaroids, marker notes, a signature. Nothing should feel system-generated.
5. **Trilingual or it doesn't ship.** Every word exists in en/es/nl; English is the canonical voice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere (existing pattern: collapse animations to opacity or nothing). Keyboard: skip link, focus-visible rings (tattoo-red on cream). Custom cursor only on fine pointers; touch gets native behaviour. Maintain readable contrast: ink on cream, cream on ink; opacity ramps never below ink/55 for meaningful text. Alt text in all three languages, written in the brand voice.
