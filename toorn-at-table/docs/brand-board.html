Voice & tone
Bourdain. Direct, honest, intimate.
  • Short sentences. Fragments welcome. Food is the spine of every story.
  • No em-dashes, anywhere. A full stop and a new sentence instead. Enforced on every change.
  • Always trilingual together: { en, es, nl }. English canonical, Spanish Andalusian, Dutch plain.
“A tomato doesn’t sit at the side of the plate. A tomato is the plate.”
Don’t
Keep it hand-made.
  • No blue/purple, tech gradients, glassmorphism or neon glow.
  • No fake bold/italic on the slab display font.
  • No over-lit, over-styled stock food photos.
  • No corporate “catering” tone. One chef, one table, a memory.
